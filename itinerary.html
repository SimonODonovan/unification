4. Day by Day Itinerary
Date Day City Accommodation Planned Activities
4 Oct 2026 Sunday London Premier Inn Southwark Arrival at London Heathrow (LHR). Transfer to the hotel by Elizabeth Line rail. Rest after the flight. Short walk along the South Bank and Millennium Bridge if the arrival time allows.
5 Oct 2026 Monday London Premier Inn Southwark Borough Market. Southwark Cathedral, Winchester Palace, and the Golden Hinde (free to view). Afternoon tea at The Swan at Shakespeare's Globe.
6 Oct 2026 Tuesday London Premier Inn Southwark Westminster Abbey (timed entry). Horse Guards. St James's Park, Buckingham Palace exterior, Green Park. Natural History Museum, South Kensington (free entry). Covent Garden in the evening.
7 Oct 2026 Wednesday London Premier Inn Southwark St Paul's Cathedral (timed entry). Postman's Park, Leadenhall Market, and St Dunstan in the East. Tower of London (timed entry). Tower Bridge and the Garden at 120.
8 Oct 2026 Thursday London Premier Inn Southwark Thames Clipper river boat to Greenwich. Cutty Sark. Greenwich Foot Tunnel. Royal Observatory and the Prime Meridian. Greenwich Park. Evening theatre performance at the Gillian Lynne Theatre.
9 Oct 2026 Friday London Premier Inn Southwark Borough Market. Warner Bros. Studio Tour, The Making of Harry Potter, at Leavesden. Travel by rail from Euston via Watford Junction and return the same day.
10 Oct 2026 Saturday London Premier Inn Southwark Kew Gardens. Lunch at Petersham Nurseries. Richmond Park and Richmond Hill (free). Return to central London by rail the same day.
11 Oct 2026 Sunday London Premier Inn Southwark Columbia Road Flower Market. Brick Lane and Old Spitalfields Market. British Museum (free entry). Hampstead Heath and Parliament Hill (free).
12 Oct 2026 Monday London / Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Check out of the London hotel. LNER rail from London King's Cross to Edinburgh Waverley, approx. 4 hrs 20 min. Check in. Royal Mile orientation walk, the Old Town closes, and the Grassmarket.
13 Oct 2026 Tuesday Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Edinburgh Castle (timed entry). St Giles' Cathedral (free). Victoria Street, The Vennel, and Greyfriars Kirkyard (free). National Museum of Scotland (free). Calton Hill viewpoint (free).
14 Oct 2026 Wednesday Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Arthur's Seat hike, Holyrood Park (free). Holyrood Palace and Abbey ruins from the exterior. Spa afternoon at One Spa, Sheraton Grand Edinburgh. Princes Street Gardens and the New Town.
15 Oct 2026 Thursday Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Royal Yacht Britannia at Leith. Lunch in Stockbridge. Water of Leith Walkway and Dean Village (free). Final evening in Edinburgh.
16 Oct 2026 Friday Edinburgh / London Premier Inn King's Cross Check out of the Edinburgh hotel. LNER rail from Edinburgh Waverley to London King's Cross, approx. 4 hrs 20 min. Check in near King's Cross. Coal Drops Yard in the evening.
17 Oct 2026 Saturday London (depart) Checked out Check out of the hotel. Piccadilly line from King's Cross St Pancras to London Heathrow (LHR), approx. 50 minutes. Return flight to Manila (MNL), Philippines. The applicant is back at work on Monday, 19 October 2026.
