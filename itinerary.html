4. Day by Day Itinerary
Date Day City Accommodation Planned Activities
4 Oct 2026 Sunday London Premier Inn Southwark Arrival at London Heathrow (LHR). Transfer to the hotel by Elizabeth Line rail. Rest after the flight. Short walk along the South Bank and Millennium Bridge if the arrival time allows.
5 Oct 2026 Monday London Premier Inn Southwark Westminster: Houses of Parliament, St James's Park, Buckingham Palace exterior. Natural History Museum, South Kensington (free entry). Covent Garden in the evening.
6 Oct 2026 Tuesday London Premier Inn Southwark Tower Bridge and St Katharine Docks. Brick Lane. Shoreditch and Spitalfields Market.
7 Oct 2026 Wednesday London Premier Inn Southwark British Museum, Bloomsbury (free entry). Grant Museum of Zoology, UCL (free entry). Borough Market. South Bank walk to Shakespeare's Globe exterior.
8 Oct 2026 Thursday London / Windsor Premier Inn Southwark Day trip by rail to Windsor, 29 miles west of London. Windsor Castle (timed entry). Windsor Great Park and the Long Walk. Return to London by rail the same day.
9 Oct 2026 Friday London Premier Inn Southwark Thames Clipper river boat to Greenwich. Royal Observatory and the Prime Meridian. Lunch at Greenwich Market. Cutty Sark.
10 Oct 2026 Saturday York Roomzzz York Check out of the London hotel. LNER rail from London King's Cross to York, approx. 2 hours. Check in. The Shambles medieval street. York City Walls walk (free).
11 Oct 2026 Sunday York Roomzzz York York Minster and tower climb. National Railway Museum (free entry). Betty's Tea Rooms, St Helen's Square. Spa afternoon at The Grand Hotel and Spa York.
12 Oct 2026 Monday York Roomzzz York Jorvik Viking Centre. Yorkshire Museum and Museum Gardens, including the St Mary's Abbey ruins. Clifford's Tower viewpoint.
13 Oct 2026 Tuesday York / Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Check out of the York hotel. LNER rail from York to Edinburgh Waverley, approx. 2 hrs 30 min. Check in. Royal Mile orientation walk, the Old Town closes, and the Grassmarket.
14 Oct 2026 Wednesday Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Edinburgh Castle (timed entry). St Giles' Cathedral (free). Victoria Street and Greyfriars Kirkyard (free). National Museum of Scotland, Chambers Street (free entry), including the natural history and animal world galleries. Calton Hill viewpoint at sunset (free).
15 Oct 2026 Thursday Edinburgh Radisson Blu Edinburgh Arthur's Seat hike, Holyrood Park (free). Holyrood Palace and Abbey ruins from the exterior. Spa afternoon at One Spa, Sheraton Grand Edinburgh. Princes Street Gardens and the New Town.
16 Oct 2026 Friday Edinburgh / London Premier Inn King's Cross Check out of the Edinburgh hotel. Royal Yacht Britannia at Leith, morning slot. LNER rail from Edinburgh Waverley to London King's Cross in the early afternoon, approx. 4 hrs 30 min. Check in near King's Cross.
17 Oct 2026 Saturday London (depart) Checked out Check out of the hotel. Piccadilly line from King's Cross St Pancras to London Heathrow (LHR), approx. 50 minutes. Depart the United Kingdom on a flight to Dublin (DUB), Ireland.
