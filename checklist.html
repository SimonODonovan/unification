The Restaurant is off on price. Le Café du Marché booked: Fri 9 Oct, 7:00pm, table for two. 22 Charterhouse Mews, Charterhouse Square EC1M 6DX, booked through OpenTable in Simon's name
Ring 020 7608 1609 a week or so beforehand — the OpenTable form carries none of the things that actually matter here. (1) Is the table ours for the evening, or is there a second sitting? Ask this one outright. A 7pm Friday table is exactly the sort that gets turned at nine, and the whole point is a long unhurried dinner. (2) A quiet table in the main room, against a wall or in a corner, away from the mews door and away from the kitchen. (3) Within earshot of the piano but not beside it. (4) Neither of you eats offal or raw shellfish — the menu changes daily and this is classical French, so foie gras, kidneys, sweetbreads and oysters are all in normal rotation, and it is far better flagged now than discovered at the table. Worth mentioning too, and true: it is Jenifer's first trip to the UK and this is the one special dinner of the fortnight. Pianist from 7:30pm, kitchen winds down about 9:30pm. Leave the hotel at 6:25pm.
