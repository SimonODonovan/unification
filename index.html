North to York

Day 10 13 Oct Tuesday
All of York, on foot
9:30amYork Minster at opening, before the coaches. The Great East Window is the largest single expanse of medieval stained glass in the world, and the Central Tower climb is 275 steps to a view over every roof in the city. Book the tower slot separately — it sells out.
Late AMStraight out of the Minster and up onto the city walls at Bootham Bar. The Bootham to Monk Bar stretch looks down on the Minster and is the best half-mile of wall in England. Full circuit is two miles and two gentle hours.
LunchOff the wall at Monk Bar and into Shambles Market, where the covered street-food row does a dozen kitchens under a tenner. Eat standing up.
AfternoonPick one. York Castle Museum has a full Victorian street rebuilt indoors and Clifford’s Tower is sixty seconds away. Or the free National Railway Museum, or JORVIK.
4:30pmMuseum Gardens — ten acres of botanical garden with the ruins of St Mary’s Abbey standing in it. Free, quiet, and at its best in October when the light goes low through the trees. Sit down.
EveningA stretch of wall at dusk as they light the Minster, then Skosh on Micklegate — the best cooking in York, a small room, and the one booking that will be gone if you wait.

Day 12 15 Oct Thursday
Castle to summit, all downhill
9:30amEdinburgh Castle at the doors — 9:30 to 11 is the only quiet window, and it matters in a school holiday week. A fortress rather than a palace: gun batteries, the Honours of Scotland, and St Margaret’s Chapel, a tiny room from about 1130 and the oldest building in Edinburgh. The Stone of Destiny is not here any more — it moved to Perth in 2024.
Late AMOut of the gates and straight down the Royal Mile. Detour down Victoria Street into the Grassmarket, up The Vennel steps for the best castle photograph in the city, and into Greyfriars Kirkyard.
LunchGrassmarket or back up on the Mile. Take your time — the afternoon is a climb, not a schedule.
2:30pmKeep walking down and the Mile delivers you to Holyrood at the foot of Arthur’s Seat — an extinct volcano, 251m, 45 minutes up by the eastern path. Afternoon beats morning: by three the October light is low and gold across the whole city. Be starting down by 5.
EveningThe one dinner you book. The Devil’s Advocate, down a close off the Royal Mile in a converted Victorian pump house — stone, low light, several hundred whiskies. Intimate rather than grand.

Visa application, preparation guide (completed)

📄  Application complete, visa granted 16 Aug 2026. Kept as a record of what was submitted
Flight tickets, do not book before the visa is granted

UK Visas and Immigration does not require confirmed or paid flight tickets as part of the application. A proposed itinerary showing intended dates, airline, and route is sufficient. The Home Office specifically advises against purchasing non-refundable tickets before a decision, as processing delays or a refusal would result in financial loss. Book flights only after the visa stamp is in the passport.

Sponsorship letter, Simon (most important document)

Simon should write a signed letter on plain paper (with full name, address, phone, and email at the top) addressed to the Entry Clearance Officer. It should clearly state his full name and nationality, his relationship to Jenifer, that he is covering all costs of the trip (flights, accommodation, transport, activities, and day-to-day expenses), an approximate total cost figure, and that he has sufficient funds to cover this without hardship. It should also state the UK departure date of 17 October 2026 and where Jenifer travels next. Only write what is true on the day it is signed. The letter, this itinerary, and anything said at the border must all say the same thing.

Simon’s bank statements

3 months of statements. The caseworker is looking for a regular salary credit each month, a healthy average balance comfortably above the total trip cost, and no large unexplained outflows. Include 2 to 3 recent payslips alongside the statements.

Jenifer’s bank statements

Jenifer should still submit her own 3-month bank statements even though Simon is the sponsor. These are not submitted to show she is self-funding, they are evidence of stable employment and financial ties to the Philippines. The key thing the caseworker wants to see is a regular IBM salary credit arriving each month. The account balance is secondary when sponsorship is clearly documented.

If a minimum balance transfer is made

If you decide to transfer funds into Jenifer’s account to show a minimum balance, the transfer should happen at least 4 weeks before the application is submitted and the funds should remain in the account. A large deposit made immediately before submission can raise suspicion. The transfer must be accompanied by a signed letter from Simon confirming the amount transferred, that it originates from his personal account, and the reason for the transfer. Include both Simon’s bank statement showing the debit and Jenifer’s statement showing the credit.

Consistency check

The total accommodation, activity, and transport costs in the sponsorship letter should be consistent with what is listed in the Visitor Travel Itinerary. If a caseworker totals the hotel costs from the itinerary, it should match what the letter claims. The itinerary itself is part of the financial evidence package.
