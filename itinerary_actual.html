5. Practical Notes
  • Arrival day. Manila is seven hours ahead of London in October, so 06:25 local is early afternoon body time. Nothing is planned before noon: she sleeps first. From the afternoon on, staying upright and outdoors until an early night beats going back to bed. Everything planned for the 5th is within walking distance of the hotel, and the only two bookings are a late-afternoon London Eye slot and dinner.
  • Arrival is at Heathrow Terminal 4. The Elizabeth line and the Piccadilly line both serve it, but the Piccadilly line splits and only alternate trains run to Terminal 4.
  • Departure day is built backwards from 07:05. The whole of Friday 16 October, including the airport hotel, exists to make FR3732 survivable. Her Qatar allowance was 25 kg and this leg is 20 kg, so weigh the bag in Edinburgh on the Thursday rather than at the desk on the Saturday.
  • If FR3732 does not fly. Edinburgh to Cork is a thin route, probably one rotation a day, so a cancellation cannot be fixed by waiting for the next one. The fallback is Edinburgh to Dublin, which has several flights a day, then the train from Heuston to Cork in about two and a half hours. The Border Management Unit named both Cork and Dublin in writing, so switching does not weaken the case at the desk. If the whole day goes, rebook for the 18th or 19th: the Short Stay Visa Waiver Programme requires entry to Ireland by 31 October 2026, so there is a two-week cushion.
  • The Qatar return has to be settled before travel. Ask Qatar in this order: reissue the return to depart Dublin in January; or add a Dublin to Heathrow sector to the existing ticket as a connection with bags checked through; or keep the Heathrow departure and book a separate hop, flying over a day early. Confirmed and changeable either way — both borders will ask to see it, and the January date may need moving forward to sit inside whatever period the Cork officer grants.
  • Totoro is ticketed. Gillian Lynne Theatre, 166 Drury Lane, London WC2B 5PW, nearest stations Covent Garden and Holborn. Two seats, stalls I41 and I42, for the 19:00 performance on Thursday 8 October 2026. The show runs about two and a half hours with an interval, so expect to be out around 21:40 and back at Southwark by about 22:20. The 19:00 curtain is why the dress-up dinner sits on Friday the 9th instead, and why the hotel stop to change is at 17:15.
  • The Studio Tour is ticketed and timed. Warner Bros. Studio Tour London, Studio Tour Drive, Leavesden, Watford WD25 7LR. Two Adult Advance tickets for 16:30 on Wednesday 7 October 2026, £117.00 paid in full. The e-tickets came by email and are shown on a phone or printed at the entrance. Admission is no earlier than 20 minutes before the slot, so there is nothing to gain by arriving early, and changes carry a £10 administration fee. Rail from Euston to Watford Junction and the Mullany's shuttle are not part of the ticket and are paid separately on the day.
  • At the border. The eVisa is read digitally from the passport, so Jenifer must travel on the passport it is linked to. A UKVI share code generated in the last few days before travel is worth having in case check-in staff ask.
